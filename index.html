About me

I received my Ph.D. degree from Huazhong University of Science and Technology in 2014. From 2014 to 2017, I conducted research and teaching at the National Laboratory of Radar Signal Processing, Xidian University. Since 2018, I have been with the School of Computer Science and Technology, Xidian University.

I welcome motivated undergraduate and master's who are interested in computer vision, intelligent image processing, and related research. Please contact me by e-mail with your CV.

Research

My research interests include:

  • Intelligent Visual Perception: Target Detection, Recognition, and Tracking in Infrared, Visible, and Remote-Sensing Imagery under Complex Backgrounds. Real-time Embedded Deployment for Industrial Edge AI.
  • Optical Image Restoration: Blind Deblurring/Deconvolution, Denoising, Infrared Non-uniformity Correction.
